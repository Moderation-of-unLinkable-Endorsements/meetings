- [#45](https://github.com/Moderation-of-unLinkable-Endorsements/internet-drafts/issues/45): one endorsement for multiple moderators.

### European Data Protection Supervisor discussion

- Martin reported meeting with the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS) on November 3. The main topics were the EU KIDS Act and age assurance, with MOLE/PACT also discussed.
- EDPS technical staff were familiar with Dennis' blog post and asked detailed questions about how the system works. Martin described them as aware of the work and somewhat enthusiastic.
- Martin raised uncertainty about whether anti-abuse use of MOLE would be permitted under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, concerning consent for storing information on users' devices.
- Upcoming omnibus provisions recognizing actions for security purposes were mentioned, but no concrete actions were identified.
- Martin noted potential deployment challenges in Europe under that reading. Possible paths discussed were changes through the omnibus provisions or sites obtaining consent, with consent expected to be burdensome for sites.

### Sessions

- Discussed [#151](https://github.com/Moderation-of-unLinkable-Endorsements/internet-drafts/pull/151) and [#152](https://github.com/Moderation-of-unLinkable-Endorsements/internet-drafts/pull/152), including whether to write a dedicated draft, independent of MOLE, defining how protocols should integrate sessions.
- Clients need to understand sessions independently of transport, without being tied to a moderator's supported transport or implementation, such as cookies or TLS tickets. This calls for a clear definition.
- Considered replacing the term session with continuity, delayed update, recovery, or another term. Different protocols may have different sessions.
- Deferred update was noted as part of moderator guidance, not exposed in the protocol or transport.
